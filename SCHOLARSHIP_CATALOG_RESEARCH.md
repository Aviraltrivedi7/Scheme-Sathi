## Catalog ingestion constraint

A separate `sourceUrl`, source type, source checked date, and rule confidence should be added before claiming 30–50 records are **verified**. A directory title and portal link are enough for discoverability, but not enough to encode income, academic-year, institution, gender, category, disability, or state hard gates safely. Any record without a reviewed official specification should remain “directory-listed” and must not appear in the strict eligibility checker.
